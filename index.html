Explore Coral Gables & Miami

If you're planning to spend a little extra time in South Florida, we hope you'll enjoy exploring the area. A few of our favorite nearby destinations include:

  • Miracle Mile & Giralda Plaza — Outdoor dining, cafés, boutiques, and galleries (5 minutes from Aloft; 5 minutes from the Biltmore).
  • Venetian Pool — A beautiful historic swimming pool carved from a coral rock quarry.
  • Vizcaya Museum & Gardens — A spectacular waterfront estate and gardens.
  • Fairchild Tropical Botanic Garden — One of the world's premier tropical gardens.
  • Little Havana — Cuban coffee, music, art, and authentic cuisine.
  • Coconut Grove — Waterfront parks, restaurants, and charming shops.
  • Wynwood Walls — Miami's renowned outdoor street art museum.
  • South Beach — Beaches, Art Deco architecture, shopping, and nightlife.

For more ideas, visit:

  • Greater Miami Visitor Guide
  • Explore Coral Gables
